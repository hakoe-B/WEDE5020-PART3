BookNest 

BookNest was established in 2012 by Andrew Theko who wanted more learners and students to have access to affordable school textbooks. It started as a small store that only sold second hand school textbooks for school children but today it has expanded to selling stationery and first-hand and second-hand textbooks and children’s books. 

 

The mission of BookNest is to ensure that every learner and student has access to affordable textbooks and stationery of good quality and providing them with great customer experience. 

 

The vision of BookNest is to be the top provider of textbooks nationally and even supply schools and tertiary institutions with good quality textbooks and starting free book clubs for kids in preschools. 

 

The target audience of BookNest are students in tertiary institutions, learners in primary and high school who are looking for affordable textbooks and stationery. Another target audience are teachers and lecturers who need school resources. 

 

 

WEBSITE GOALS AND OBJECTIVES 

 

The goals for this BookNest website are: 

 

To promote special sales offers. By offering specials to the customers that purchase more than a certain amount on the website to boost sales made on the website of BookNest.  

Make it easier to purchase books and stationery online. The goal is to make it easier for the user to easily navigate through the website when purchasing either books or stationery, by making each navigation and payment methods quicker, faster and more efficient and improving overall customer experience online.  
 

The website’s performance will be measured by comparing online sales with physical store sales. Its efficiency will also be evaluated through customer ratings and feedback on how easy the website is to navigate. This feedback will help developers identify and fix issues that may negatively affect the customer experience. 

 

WEBSITE ANALYSIS 

The strengths of the BookNest website are that it is always available 24/7 and customers can shop at anytime and anywhere in the country. Another strength is the easier and user-friendly navigation. The weakness includes the later product delivery to the customer because of the distance between the user and the physical store. This weakness can be improved by providing the dates the user with where they can expect their products. 

 

 

 

WEBSITE FEATURES AND FUNCTIONALITY 

The BookNest website will have the following features: 

Homepage: Welcoming the customer to the page and showing the latest products and promotions. 

About us: Webpage showing the brief history as well as their objectives, mission statements and vision. 

Products: The range of products that BookNest sells and a search bar to search for products.  

Contact us: Webpage showing the company’s contact details and the locations of the physical stores 

Enquiries: A webpage that will show the customer a form where the customer can fill in their details and type in their enquiry they have about the products or the website.  

 

DESIGN AND USER EXPERIENCE 

The website is going to fit the aesthetic of a bookstore for students, learners and kids. The colour schemes that are chosen for this website are : 

 

Cream white/ Light Yellow – For the background 

White - For the product cards 

Dark teal – For the footer and bigger text 

Yellow – For the button and navigation header 

Black – Font colour for the descriptive texts 

 

The website will make use of two fonts consistently throughout the website Roca Two and Sans this will make look aesthetically pleasing for a bookstore that sells books for students and kids
